## Original assets

These were created for RCB Wake by its author, contain no third-party material, and are covered by
the project licence and the copyright notice in [NOTICE](NOTICE):

| Asset | Location | How it was made |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alarm tones *Dawn, Chime, Pulse, Classic* | `android/app/src/main/res/raw/tone_*.wav` | Synthesised from sine waves by `scripts/generate-tones.py` — no samples or recordings used |
| App (launcher) icon | `res/drawable/ic_launcher_*.xml` | Drawn from basic shapes by `scripts/generate-app-icons.py` |
| Weather icons | `res/drawable/ic_wx_*.xml` | Drawn from basic shapes by `scripts/generate-weather-icons.py` |
| Interface icons and settings gear | `src/components/Icon.tsx`, `src/components/gearPath.ts` | Hand-written SVG paths; gear generated by `scripts/generate-app-icons.py` |
| Notification icons, floral accent, ringing screen, widget layouts, themes | `res/`, `src/` | Original designs |

Sounds that are **not** part of the app and therefore need no licence here: the *System default*
alarm sound (it is the phone's own ringtone, played from the device) and any custom sound a user
picks (the user's own file, read in place and never copied).
