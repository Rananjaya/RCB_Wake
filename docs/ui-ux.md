# UI and UX specification

## Principles

Minimal, calm and legible at a glance — including half-asleep. Large clock type, rounded cards,
consistent spacing, no clutter, subtle motion, accessible contrast.

## Themes

screen matches).

Pink Dream adds a small five-petal floral accent (toolbar, empty states, About).

### Accent colours

Settings → Accent colour offers *Theme default* plus purple, blue, teal, green, orange and rose.
Each accent has a dark-theme and light-theme variant (`src/theme/themes.ts`) with its own
on-accent text colour for contrast. Yellow and gold are deliberately excluded.

### App icon

Settings → App icon offers eight launcher icons (purple default, blue, teal, green, orange, rose,
midnight, cream), generated by `scripts/generate-app-icons.py`. Each is an `<activity-alias>`;
exactly one is enabled. Launchers may take a few seconds to refresh.

### Onboarding

First launch shows a versioned privacy agreement (English, agree or exit) and then a five-page
tutorial. No permission is requested until onboarding is complete.

## Spacing and shape

Spacing scale 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32. Radii 10 / 16 / 24 / pill. Minimum touch target 40–56 dp.

## Navigation

Bottom tabs: **Alarm · Timer · Stopwatch · World Clock**. Settings is the gear in every tab's
toolbar. Modal screens: alarm editor (modal), city picker, Settings, About, math challenge
(full-screen, no back gesture).

## Home-screen widget

Frameless — text and icons sit directly on the wallpaper with a soft shadow for legibility.
Layouts by size: **bar** (time + weather), **small** (stacked hour/minute + date), **medium**
(time, date, weather), **wide** (time + next alarm | weather + date), **large** (time, date +
weather, next alarm). Weather icons are original 3D-style artwork (gradient sun, translucent cloud,
moon, rain, snow, storm, fog, showers).

The clock size is computed from the widget size (several size-map entries per layout), so it grows with the widget while the date stays directly under it. On Android 12+ the launcher picks the layout from a size
map; launchers that do not report exact sizes (Xiaomi HyperOS, many third-party launchers) get one
layout chosen from the reported minimum width and height. Text colour (Settings → Home-screen
widget) is white, Material You (wallpaper `system_accent` tones, Android 12+, follows wallpaper
changes live) or the app's accent colour.

### My Day widget

A second widget (`GlanceWidgetProvider`) with two pages in a `ViewFlipper`:

1. Optional greeting by time of day (morning 05–12, afternoon 12–17, evening 17–21, night 21–05),
   the date with year (large when the greeting is off), and the weather icon, temperature and place.
2. "Coming up": the next calendar event, the next alarm and connected earphones (optional).

Pages switch on a tap, and optionally every 6 seconds (Settings → Switch My Day pages
automatically, off by default); a double tap opens the app (taps go through the invisible `WidgetTapActivity`, which measures
the time between them). Each page carries its own vertical page dots on the right. From 10 minutes
before a timed calendar event or an alarm until it starts (events: until they end), the widget
jumps once to "Soon" on page 2 with only the item(s) that are soon (plus connected earphones);
pages then switch automatically even if that setting is off, taps keep working, and "Soon" (and
its automatic switching) ends when the item is over. Launchers use horizontal swipes to change home-screen pages and widgets cannot receive them,
so a tap is used instead of a swipe. If page 2 has nothing to show, the widget has one page and a
tap opens the app. The greeting is refreshed at each boundary by an inexact `RTC` alarm.

## Localisation

All UI strings go through `useT()`; native strings through `res/values*/strings.xml`. City names
are English. Times follow the device's 12/24-hour setting by default (Settings → Time format → Use device
setting); users can force 12- or 24-hour. The widget's `TextClock` switches live with the device.

## Accessibility

Buttons and switches carry roles, labels and states; live regions announce the next alarm,
challenge feedback and countdowns; the clock face is announced as text. Day chips use full day
names as labels.
