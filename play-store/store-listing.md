## Full description (max 4000 characters)

RCB Wake is a calm, reliable alarm clock for Android. Alarms are scheduled with Android's
alarm-clock system and handled by a native engine, so they ring with the app closed, with the
screen off, after a restart, and across time-zone and clock changes.

ALARMS
• One-time or repeating alarms with labels
• Built-in tones or your own audio file, vibration, gradual volume and snooze length
• Full-screen ringing screen shown over the lock screen
• "Next alarm" shown in the status bar while an alarm is set

WAKE-UP CHALLENGE
• Solve easy, medium or hard math questions before the alarm can be dismissed
• Or scan any QR code — for example on a product in another room
• An emergency dismiss (press and hold) is always available

TIMERS AND FOCUS
• Several countdown timers at once, with labels and quick presets
• Pomodoro focus timer with short and long breaks and auto-start
• Stopwatch with laps; fastest and slowest laps highlighted

WORLD CLOCK
• About 100 cities with day/night and time difference, daylight-saving aware

BEDTIME REMINDER
• A gentle reminder to go to bed, based on your next alarm and your sleep goal

HOME-SCREEN WIDGET
• Frameless, resizable clock with date and next alarm
• Text in white, Material You colours (Android 12+) or the app's accent colour
• Optional current weather from Open-Meteo, or from OpenWeatherMap with your own API key,
  updated at the interval you choose
• Optional next calendar event from the calendars you pick
• Second widget, "My Day": a greeting that changes through the day, the date, weather and
  your next event

MAKE IT YOURS
• Midnight Purple and Pink Dream themes, accent colours and alternative app icons
• Follows your phone's 12/24-hour setting
• English, Sinhala, Tamil, German, Russian, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Hindi,
  Chinese (Simplified) and Japanese

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking
• Your alarms, timers, cities and settings stay on your phone and are excluded from cloud backup
• The internet is used only for optional features you turn on (widget weather and place search)
• Location, calendar and camera are requested only for the optional features that need them

RCB Wake is source-available and free for personal, non-commercial use.

## Privacy summary

RCB Wake collects no personal data and has no server. Alarms, timers, cities and settings are
stored only on the device and excluded from backup. Optional features send data only when turned
on: widget weather sends rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo (or, with the user's own key, to
OpenWeatherMap); place search sends the typed text to Open-Meteo; "Use my location" may use
Android's geocoder for the place name. Camera images (QR challenge) and calendar events never leave
the device. Full policy: PRIVACY_POLICY.md.
